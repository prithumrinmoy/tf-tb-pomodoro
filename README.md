# tf-tb-pomodoro

tf-tb-pomodoro combines the proven Pomodoro time-management technique with a private, local music player to help you stay locked in during study sessions, coding, or deep work. Instead of juggling separate browser tabs or dealing with streaming ads, you can upload your own audio tracks directly into the app and manage your focus intervals in one clean workspace.

Key Features
Customizable Pomodoro Timer: Alternate seamlessly between focused work intervals and short/long breaks to prevent burnout.

Private Local Music Player: Upload and play your own personal audio files (MP3, WAV, OGG, etc.) directly in the app—no accounts, subscriptions, or external streaming required.

Distraction-Free Study Environment: Keep your timer and study playlist in a single window so you never lose focus switching tabs.

How It Works
Upload Your Music: Select and load your private audio files from your device into the built-in player.

Set Your Timer: Choose your focus and break durations.

Press Play & Focus: Start the Pomodoro countdown while your personal soundtrack plays in the background.
